About the Project

Project Overview

The Smart Virtual Queue System is a web-based queue management concept designed to reduce physical waiting lines.

Instead of standing in a queue, users can join the queue virtually and monitor their token status through the system.

Problem Statement

Traditional queue systems require people to physically stand in line for long periods. This results in wasted time, overcrowding and inconvenience.

Objectives

  • Reduce physical waiting time.
  • Provide virtual queue tokens.
  • Allow users to monitor queue status.
  • Help administrators manage queues.
  • Improve overall service efficiency.

Advantages

  • Simple and user-friendly.
  • Reduces crowding.
  • Saves users' time.
  • Organizes the queue efficiently.
  • Can be used in hospitals, banks and offices.

Future Scope

  • JavaScript-based live queue updates.
  • Database integration.
  • SMS and email notifications.
  • Mobile application.
  • Real-time admin dashboard.
  • Online appointment scheduling.
